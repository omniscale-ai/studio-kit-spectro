# FIT — Review Checklist

- [ ] Is the model given as an equation with all free parameters named?
- [ ] Is the weighting written out and licensed by a CALIBRATION?
- [ ] Is the effective sample size reported, and is it a sane fraction of the
      points used?
- [ ] Are parameters on a bound flagged as such?
- [ ] Do the points used match the working window from the artefact scan?
- [ ] Is the fit reproducible from the Attestation alone?
