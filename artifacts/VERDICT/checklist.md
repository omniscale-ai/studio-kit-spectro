# VERDICT — Review Checklist

- [ ] Does a permitting verdict address all five criteria?
- [ ] Does every threshold resolve to a CALIBRATION artifact?
- [ ] Is identifiability judged separately from goodness of fit?
- [ ] If refused: is it stated whether the parameter is biased or imprecise?
- [ ] Is the misfit statistic one whose calibration shows it separating good
      fits from bad on independently judged cases?
- [ ] Is the signal inside the instrument's declared trusted range?
- [ ] Was this record produced by a script, and left unedited?
