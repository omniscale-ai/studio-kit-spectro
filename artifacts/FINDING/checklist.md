# FINDING — Review Checklist

- [ ] Do all cited verdicts permit the parameters the claim uses?
- [ ] Is the claimed axis MEASUREMENT or SYNTHESIS — and does the claim's form
      (rate law, activation energy, response) match?
- [ ] Does any geometric factor vary along the claimed axis? How large is it
      compared with the claimed effect?
- [ ] Are all measurements in the trusted instrument range?
- [ ] Is there selection: did the surviving measurements survive for a reason
      correlated with the claim?
- [ ] Is within-group scatter reported alongside the between-group effect?
- [ ] Does it reproduce in an independent set? Same sign?
